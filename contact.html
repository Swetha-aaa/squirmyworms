Found a bug, want a song added, or just want to say hi? We'd love to hear from you.

hello@squirmyworms.com

Good things to write about

  • Bugs. Tell us what you were doing, what went wrong, and which device and browser you were using.
  • Song requests. Name the song and artist, and which mix it belongs in.
  • Wrong answers. If a song's title, artist or year looks wrong, let us know.
  • Rights holders. If you'd like a clip removed, include the song and your details, and we'll take it down promptly.
  • Privacy requests. Ask what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it. See the Privacy Policy.

We usually reply within a few days.
