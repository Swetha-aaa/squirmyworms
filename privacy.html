Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

SquirmyWorms is a free browser game. We don't have accounts, and we try to collect as little as possible. This page explains what is collected, by whom, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

Duel name. When you play a duel, the name you type is sent to our server so your opponent can see it. It's kept in memory only while the room is open and is not saved to a database.

Messages. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply.

Information stored on your device

The game uses your browser's local storage to remember things between visits:

  • your best scores and the mix you last chose
  • a list of recently played songs, so you don't get the same ones again too soon (this list is sent to our server when a new round is picked)
  • your duel name, and a temporary key that lets you reconnect to a duel if your page reloads

You can clear all of this at any time by clearing your browser's site data for squirmyworms.com.

Information collected automatically

Hosting. Our hosting provider, Render, automatically keeps standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and the time of each request, for security and to keep the service running.

Analytics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views and see roughly where visitors come from. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't track individuals across sites. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads, which helps keep the game free.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.
  • Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

How we use information

To run the game and duels, keep the site secure, understand how many people visit, show ads, and reply to messages. We don't sell your personal data.

Children

SquirmyWorms is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can clear local storage, block cookies in your browser, and opt out of personalised ads using the links above. Depending on where you live, including under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act, you may have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, or to ask us to correct or delete it. Email hello@squirmyworms.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hello@squirmyworms.com.
