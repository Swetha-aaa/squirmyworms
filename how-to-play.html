How to play

Four songs play at the same time. Your job is to pick each one out of the noise and name it before the clock runs away from you.

The basics

  1. Choose a mix. Pick Pop or K-pop on the home page. Every round draws four songs from that mix, each by a different artist.
  2. Press play. All four 30-second clips start together. The timer starts with the music.
  3. Listen for one song at a time. Lock onto a voice, a hook or a beat, then type the title or artist into a slot and pick the match from the list.
  4. Lock it in. A right answer stays in its slot. A wrong one clears the slot and costs you points.
  5. Find all four. The round ends when every song is named, or after three wrong guesses, when the mix is revealed.

Scoring

Each song you name100 points
All four songs named+250 bonus
Wrong guess−25 points

You also get a time bonus based on how long the whole round took, counted from the moment the music started. It's scaled by how many songs you found, so rushing in with wild guesses earns nothing.

A perfect, lightning-fast round is worth about 1,050 points.

Ranks

Your points decide your listener rank at the end of each round.

  • 0+ Shower singer
  • 100+ Casual radio listener
  • 300+ Playlist curator
  • 500+ Front-row fan
  • 700+ Human Shazam
  • 900+ Secret pop star

Duel a friend

Duels are one round, head to head, on the same four songs.

  1. Pick a mix, then create a room and send your friend the code or invite link. You can also tap Random to be matched with another player online.
  2. Both players tap Ready. After a short countdown the same mix plays for both of you.
  3. First to lock in a song claims it. It shows up on your opponent's screen with your name next to it, and they can't take it.
  4. Wrong guesses freeze you for two seconds, so guess carefully.
  5. The round ends when all four songs are claimed, or when both players agree to end it early.
  6. Most songs wins. The same number is a draw. Then ask for a rematch, or find someone new.

After the round

The results page reveals all four songs. Use Listen back to play any of them on their own, or layer two, three or all four together to hear how the mix was built. It's the best way to train your ear for next time.

Tips

  • Start with the song that's easiest to hear, often the one with the clearest vocals, then look for what's left underneath.
  • Drag the white playhead to replay a section. Choruses usually give songs away fastest.
  • Type the artist if you can't remember the title. The list will narrow down quickly.
  • Each round has four different artists, so once you've found one song by an artist, the other three are by someone else.

About SquirmyWorms
